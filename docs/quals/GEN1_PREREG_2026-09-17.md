## Target weakness (chosen from measured evidence, Lane E)

The Gen-0 diagnostics (16 prompts × 128 tokens, greedy, chat template) show:

- EOS termination rate **0.000**; max-token-cap hit rate **1.000**.
- **12/16 completions cut off mid-plan with an unclosed `<think>`**; 4/16
  closed `</think>` and emitted the correct final answer but still never
  reached EOS within 128 tokens.
- Zero literal loops (distinct-trigram 0.988) — plain repetition is NOT the
  failure; **turn termination is**.
- Role-marker imitation (`assistant`, `<think>` as literal content): the
  abliterated base model imitates the chat template rather than being tuned
  to it.

**Gen-1 target: chat-protocol compliance — the model ends its turn (EOS)
and closes its thinking block within budget.** This target was chosen over
the raw "non-termination" reading because the evidence shows partial
competence (4 correct short answers) plus budget exhaustion, not a
degenerate loop. It is also upstream of every capability measurement: a
model that never ends its turn cannot be graded reliably on anything.

## Training data (contamination-safe by construction)

Smallest curriculum that tests the hypothesis, roles per CurriculumEngine:

- **TARGET** (~70%): synthetic termination-format examples — short
  instruction → brief correct answer → EOS, and think-style prompts →
  closed `</think>` → answer → EOS. Template-generated by
  `chowder.growth.synthetic`, programmatically verified (format + answer
  checked by construction → trust class GOLD).
- **PRESERVE** (~15%): replay-format short QA over the target template
  family with varied topics (anti-overfit of the termination behavior to
  one topic). No measured protected behavior exists to preserve beyond
  this; declared.
- **GENERAL** (~15%): template-generated short-instruction examples,
  varied imperative forms.
- **REPLAY / STRETCH**: empty (nothing banked, nothing affordable) — declared.

Every source is registered in the `DataRegistry` with license (authored
in-repo, Apache-2.0 project), content manifest, and a firewall check
**before admission**; KNOWN/POSSIBLE contamination refuses eligibility.
No benchmark test examples anywhere in training material.

## Budgets and stopping rules (frozen)

- Training device ceiling: **0.25 GPU-h** per recipe (aggregate 0.50).

  refusal (production path behavior).

## Promotion rule (frozen, mechanical)

`evaluate_promotion` decides. Hard requirements: target improves by ≥ 0.90
absolute on the instrument score; protected regressions ≤ 0.02; candidate
artifact/evaluation identities reconcile via `MetricBinder` (wrong
generation, unregistered benchmark, or self-supplied anchors refuse).
A REJECTED verdict is an acceptable, durable outcome. The promoted or
rejected candidate is recorded in the `GenerationLedger` with full
provenance either way.
